Night-market zone (B1-B50) monster / trap / boss sound effects: z0_*.mp3
========================================================================

Every file is a layered mix of real recordings: 2 to 6 layers that are varispeeded, filtered,
enveloped, offset in time and loudness-matched (no synthesizer, no AI generation). The mix is made by
_build/tools/sfx/make_z0.py; durations and descriptions are in _build/tools/sfx/z0_meta.json.
