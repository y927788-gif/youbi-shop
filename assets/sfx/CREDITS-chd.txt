鎖鏈舞者・綾 (Chain Dancer · Aya) sound effects: cd*.mp3
=========================================================

Every file is a layered mix of real recordings. Each one stacks 1 to 4 layers that are varispeeded,
filtered, offset in time, enveloped and loudness-matched. The mix is made by
_build/tools/sfx/make_chd.py.

All sources are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) from OpenGameArt.org. Attribution is not required;
the authors are credited here as a courtesy.
