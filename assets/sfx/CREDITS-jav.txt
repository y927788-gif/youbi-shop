Dragonflame Spear (javelin cosmetic) sound effects: jvt2*.mp3 (stage-2 throw), jvt3*.mp3 (stage-3 throw burst),
jvig*.mp3 (charge stage-up ignition), jvlp.mp3 (looping fire roar while charging),
jvhs*.mp3 / jvhb*.mp3 (stage-2 / stage-3 hit explosion), jvwl*.mp3 (hitting a wall or the ground)
=====================================================================================================

Every file is a layered mix of real recordings (varispeed, filtering, envelopes, loudness matching),
made with _build/tools/sfx/make_jav.py. No synthesizers, no AI generation.
