刪除夜市地下城帳號

在遊戲裡刪除（最快）：打開夜市地下城 →「造型商城」→「帳號」→「刪除帳號」，確認兩次就會立即刪除。

在這裡刪除：輸入帳號的救援碼（在「造型商城 → 帳號」可以看到），確認後立即刪除。

會刪除的資料

  • 遊戲帳號編號、救援碼、Google 登入綁定
  • 剩下的金燈籠、🏮 造型、雲端存檔
  • 購物回饋的訂單編號登記

會保留的資料

  • 付款與退款紀錄（依法保留 7 年，用於會計與退款），刪除後不再連到任何帳號
  • 你自己裝置上的遊戲進度不會被刪，要清掉請到瀏覽器或 App 設定清除網站資料

忘記救援碼又沒綁 Google：請寄信到客服信箱或用遊戲裡的「回饋」（類別選「其他」），寫下帳號編號前 8 碼，我們會在 7 天內刪除。

Delete your Night Market Dungeon account

In the game (fastest): open Night Market Dungeon → Style Shop → Account → Delete account, and confirm twice. It is deleted immediately.

On this page: enter your recovery code (shown in Style Shop → Account) above and confirm.

Deleted: account ID, recovery code, Google sign-in link, remaining Gold Lanterns, 🏮 outfits, cloud save and rebate claims. Kept: payment and refund records (kept 7 years as required for accounting and refunds), no longer linked to any account. Progress stored on your own device is not touched; clear site data in your browser or app settings to remove it.

Lost your recovery code and no Google sign-in? Send in-game feedback (category “Other”) or email us with the first 8 characters of your account ID; we delete it within 7 days.
