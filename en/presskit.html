Night Market Dungeon — Press Kit

When the night market closes, the dungeon underneath wakes up. Night Market Dungeon is a pixel action roguelite set under a Taiwanese night market: grab a spatula, an umbrella or your phone and fight your way down, collect legendary weapons, run your own night-market manor, and work a 3D sausage stall — solo or with up to 4 players online. It runs right in your phone or desktop browser, no download needed — and it is also on the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android).

Features

  • Snappy pixel action: melee, ranged and a 3-stage charged throw; time your parry to send bullets back; smash the walls.
  • Legendary weapons: every legendary weapon has its own skill, plus the Golden Dragon ultimate.
  • Lots to play: Story Mode, a 50-floor Adventure Mode plus the endless Abyss, Guardian Trials and a Daily Challenge.
  • 3D sausage stall (new): flip, fan the fire and glaze; residents you know drop by to chat. Run it together with 2–4 players.
  • Online co-op for up to 4: join friends by room code or quick match; chat is safety-filtered.
  • Manor & pets: build your own night-market manor, raise pets and befriend the residents.
  • Fair monetization: upgrades and star-ups always succeed; paid items are outfits and pets only.
